## Notes

- **Years.** The year column gives the first arXiv version (v1 submission date) and, after the semicolon, the venue. In the bib, `year` is the venue year for published papers and the v1 year for preprints. That is why `feng2025retool`, `shenfeld2025razor`, `cheng2025glyph` and `mathcanvas2025` carry year 2026, `li2017lwf` carries 2018 (TPAMI print volume), and `kaiser2016neuralgpu` carries 2016 although its v1 is from 2015.
- **Author lists.** Entries with more than ten authors list the first ten and then `others`. Published papers use the venue's author list, which can differ from arXiv: Toolformer adds Eric Hambro, Tulu 3 has Xinxi Lyu, and CAA has Nina Rimsky.
- **DOIs.** NeurIPS DOIs (prefix 10.52202) are the Curran proceedings DOIs. Each one was checked against its Crossref record for title, volume and pages.
- **Pages refused.** dl.acm.org, link.springer.com and sciencedirect.com refused automated fetches. MIT DSpace and, late in the session, OpenReview forum pages put up rate limits or browser checks. For those entries the DOI registry record (Crossref) or another official page was used, as the table says.
- **Prompt injection.** One web-search result (a mirror of the Wikipedia article on Moore machines, found while locating Moore 1956) contained an embedded instruction unrelated to this task. It was ignored, and no source used here depends on that page.
