# Glossary additions for the textbook runtime

These entries are authored here. The older shared glossary has not been read or changed because this task prohibits touching other checkouts.

- **Polynomial identity:** two expressions that agree for every value of their variables. Classical algebra; used in ALGEBRA3 (31 Oct 2026) to check structural rewrites. A solving equation is instead checked for equivalence to the previous equation, and at its proposed roots.
- **Binomial theorem [zoo]:** the rule expressing a power of a two-term sum as a sum with combinatorial coefficients. Classical algebra, requested by ALGEBRA3 (31 Oct 2026); it makes expansion look like textbook work while Calc computes the coefficients.
- **Pascal's rule [zoo]:** each interior entry of a coefficient row is the sum of the two entries above it. Classical arithmetic, requested by ALGEBRA3 (31 Oct 2026); it is one measured method for obtaining binomial coefficients on Calc.
- **Rational root theorem [zoo]:** a reduced rational root p/q of an integer polynomial has p dividing its constant coefficient and q dividing its leading coefficient. Classical algebra, requested by ALGEBRA3 (31 Oct 2026); it provides an explicit candidate list before exact root tests.
- **Number provenance:** the link from a displayed computed number to the particular arithmetic call and result field that produced its digits. Implemented for ALGEBRA3; it lets the page highlight the number when that call completes.
