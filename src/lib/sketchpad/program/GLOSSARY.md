# Round 4 terms

- **Vieta's formulas [zoo]:** the roots of ax²+bx+c have sum −b/a and product c/a. Classical algebra associated with François Viète; requested by ALGEBRA4. Checking both identities for a proposed pair proves that pair has the given quadratic, without decimal root substitution.
- **Square-free part:** the factor left after extracting all square factors from a positive integer. Here trial primes stop at 50, so the remaining factor is only claimed square-free when the search finishes before that cap.
- **Radical pair:** the exact expressions (p ± q√m)/d. They preserve an irrational answer exactly while a separately labeled decimal approximation provides a readable estimate.
